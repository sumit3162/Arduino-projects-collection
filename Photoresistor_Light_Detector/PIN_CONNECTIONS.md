# Project 7: Photoresistor Light Detector - Pin Connections

## Components Needed
- 1 x Photoresistor (LDR)
- 1 x 10kΩ Resistor
- 1 x LED
- 1 x 220Ω Resistor
- Jumper Wires

## Connections
-   **LED**:
    -   Anode -> 220Ω Resistor -> **Pin 13**
    -   Cathode -> **GND**
-   **Photoresistor Circuit (Voltage Divider)**:
    -   One leg of the Photoresistor to **5V**.
    -   The other leg of the Photoresistor to **A0**.
    -   The same leg connected to A0 also connects to one end of the 10kΩ resistor. The other end of the resistor goes to **GND**.
