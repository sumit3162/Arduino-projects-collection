# Project 54: NeoPixel Potentiometer Control - Pin Connections

## Components Needed
- 1 x NeoPixel Strip or Ring
- 3 x 10kΩ Potentiometers
- Jumper Wires

## Library Needed
- **Adafruit NeoPixel**

## Connections
-   **NeoPixel Strip/Ring**:
    -   VCC -> 5V
    -   GND -> GND
    -   DI -> Pin 6
-   **Potentiometers**:
    -   **Red Control Potentiometer**:
        -   Center Pin -> **A0**
        -   Outer pins to 5V and GND
    -   **Green Control Potentiometer**:
        -   Center Pin -> **A1**
        -   Outer pins to 5V and GND
    -   **Blue Control Potentiometer**:
        -   Center Pin -> **A2**
        -   Outer pins to 5V and GND
